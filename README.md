## My Favorite Movie: The Shawshank Redemption

**The Shawshank Redemption** is my favorite movie. It is a *drama* about Andy Dufresne, a banker who is wrongly sentenced to life in prison and slowly builds an unlikely friendship with fellow inmate Red while never giving up hope of freedom.

### Why I Like It
- Powerful and inspiring storyline
- Unforgettable characters
- Amazing performances by Tim Robbins and Morgan Freeman
- Strong themes of hope, friendship, and perseverance

### Rating
**5/5**

> "The Shawshank Redemption reminds viewers that hope can never be taken away."
